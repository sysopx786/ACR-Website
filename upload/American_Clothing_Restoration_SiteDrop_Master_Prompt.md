# 2. WEBSITE PURPOSE

The website must accomplish four things:

1. Make restoration contractors and insurance professionals immediately understand what American Clothing Restoration does.
2. Make the company look established, focused, professional, and capable.
3. Show the value of textile restoration visually through dramatic before-and-after imagery.
4. Give homeowners and property owners confidence when another company refers American Clothing Restoration into their loss.

This website does **not** need aggressive lead generation.

It should feel like a polished digital presentation a restoration professional can open after hearing the company name and immediately think:

> “These people specialize in this work.”

The visual impression should be:
- premium
- restrained
- established
- editorial
- meticulous
- highly professional
- calm
- credible
- specialized

Avoid panic-driven disaster marketing.

Do not make the site look like SERVPRO, a franchise template, a neighborhood dry cleaner, or a generic contractor website.
